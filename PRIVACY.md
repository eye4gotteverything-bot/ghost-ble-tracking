# Privacy Policy — Ghost BLE Tracking (web version)

Last updated: 2026-11-19

## The short version

Ghost BLE Tracking's web version collects nothing, sends nothing, and tracks
nobody. Everything you do in the app stays in your own browser, on your own
device.

## What data the app stores, and where

The app saves only its own working data, in your browser's **localStorage**
on your device:

- Your settings (Demo Mode on/off, environment choice, band toggles, alert
  thresholds, the “Send updates to Muse chat” preference).
- Devices **you chose to track**, with their distance logs.
- Your **scan history** (finished sessions, their device lists and their
  Live Updates timelines).

There is no server, no account, no cloud sync, and no backup. If you clear
the browser's site data, the data is gone.

## What the app does NOT do

- It does **not** send any data to the developer or to any third party.
- It contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking** code or
  third-party trackers of any kind.
- It does **not** scan your real surroundings. A web page cannot use your
  phone's Bluetooth or Wi-Fi radios, and this app does not try to.

## Demo Mode data

The only “devices” and “networks” this web version can display come from its
Demo Mode. That data is **fictional**, generated in your browser from a small
built-in list of sample entries with made-up names and clearly fictional
addresses. It is not collected from you, your phone, or anyone around you,
and it disappears when you turn Demo Mode off.

## How to delete all of your data

Either way removes everything the app stored:

1. In the app: **Settings → Delete all app data**, or
2. In your browser: clear site data / cookies and site data for the page
   hosting the app.

Uninstalling is not required because nothing was installed and nothing left
your device.

## Children

The app contains no advertising, no purchases and no data collection, and is
not directed at children.

## The future native Android version

A separate, future **native Android build** is planned so the app can perform
real Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning. That build would need Android permissions
(Bluetooth Scan, location, and Wi-Fi scanning) and would be covered by its
**own privacy policy and Google Play Data Safety declarations before it is
published**. This policy covers the web version only, and nothing in this
web version uses or requests those permissions.
